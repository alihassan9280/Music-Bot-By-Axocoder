## 🎯 ROLES ka core (quick recap)

| Role | Position | Stats | Weapons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Attacker** | 1 | High STR ya MAG, 1-2 WP | Dagger(108), Flame(110), Bow(103), Energy(111) |
| **Healer** | 2 | MAG>WP, good HP | Spirit(112), Healing(102) |
| **Replenisher** | 3 | WP>MAG, good HP | Arcane Scepter(113) |
| **Tank** | 3 | High HP + res + WP | Shield(105), Orb(106), Banner(116) |

**Golden rules:**
- MAG weapon → MAG pet; STR weapon → STR pet. (kabhi mix mat karo)
- Har weapon ka **WP cost** sabse important (Rstaff ~300, Shield ~150, Crune ~100).
- **Mana Tap** attacker ke liye core. **Sacrifice + Rstaff** = infinite revive loop.
- **Taunt** hamesha turn ke start mein activate (position koi bhi ho).
- Banner **taunt + defup bypass** karta hai.

## ⚙️ KEY BATTLE MECHANICS (reminder)
- 3v3, 24 turns max → auto-tie. Team HP ≤ 0 = loss.
- Resistance → % = res/(100+res)×0.8, **max 80%** reduction.
- Physical ← PR, Magical ← MR, **TRUE ignores both**.
- WP < weapon cost → plain STR basic attack.
- Underleveled pet = **10× tak XP** (jaldi level up).
- XP: Lose 50, Tie 100, Win 200 + bonus.

## 👹 BOSS (quick)
- `owo battle` → 5% boss spawn → `owo boss` (1 ticket, 3/day).
- Boss = Empowered weapons, huge HP.
- **Best:** Double Poison Dagger + Giant Slayer + Celebration rune (poison = MAG true dmg, ignores resistance).
- Switch to Vampiric/Energy + Mana Tap vs Stinky/Purity bosses.
- Boss templates category: `nt v boss` | NeonUtil boss tools: `nboss`

## 🔑 STREAK BUILDING TIPS
1. **Set and forget** — ek team pe tiko, roz battle karo.
2. **Level up tanks** — low level pe meta teams weak hoti hain; high level pe 100k+.
3. Streak team **selected** rakho (`owo setteam 1`); boss ke liye alag team.
4. Dono teams ki streak **alag** count hoti hai.
5. Weapon reroll: `owo w rr <id> passive` (passive) ya `stats` (sab) — passive reroll se stats safe.
6. Simulate before switching: `ng <team>` (NeonUtil server) ya `nb` (battle test).
